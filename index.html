Etymology

Why "Atelier"?

Atelier / at·ell·ee·ay / French

The French word for a craftsman's workshop — a place where skilled makers create by hand, one piece at a time.

✦

Our watches are inspired by that philosophy. Not mass produced. Not rushed. Each watch is individually assembled, inspected and prepared for its owner.
